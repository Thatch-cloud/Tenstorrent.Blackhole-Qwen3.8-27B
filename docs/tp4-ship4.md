# tp4/ship-4: the combined ship candidate

Branch `tp4/ship-4` = `tp4/octo-3` (the fusion ship stack, the prefill levers, octo-T8 and its three levers, the tile-aligned drafter window, the tool-call whitespace fix) + `tp4/prefix-tiers` (the host KV tier,
the preconverted GDN checkpoints, the tenant quotas, version 2 of the `qwen_kv_read` extension) + the work below. It is the **one candidate** the owner can decide on instead of five.
Everything here is **default off, gate only or under ONE owner traffic waiver (PENDING), and unqualified on cards**. Nothing in this document has run on a card **together**.

## 1. What the candidate is

`c2-packed-tp4-8x262k-ship-prefix-levern-w2-er-fx-traffic-s4` (a TRAFFIC profile, 59 characters: a baked default name is at most 64) is generated by `make_fusion_profiles.py` from the ship entry `traffic-s4`
of `scripts/ci/fusion-wp/S4.json`. It is the approved fusion ship profile (`-fx-traffic`, byte-identical, with its waiver) plus exactly:

| part | env | what it is |
| --- | --- | --- |
| prefill SDPA, one q chunk per core | `QWEN_FAST_SDPA_PF_ONEQ=1` | acts in prefill only; the image bakes the K64j-OQ graft (`bake_graft`), whose packed-any records have landed (tp4/oneq-ship) |
| 13x10 matmul grids | `QWEN_FAST_GRID13=prefill,drafter` | **the one switchable line** (section 4); the decode verify sub-switch (`verify.out`) is held out of the candidate and runs only in the audited twin (`=1`) until an audited attach has compared it |
| octo-T8, non-alternating | `QWEN_FAST_OCTO=live`, `_OCTO_MIN_LIVE=6`, `_OCTO_DRAFT=1`, `_OCTO_ATTN_BUNDLE=4`, `_OCTO_GLUE8=1`, `QWEN_FAST_OCTO_WAIVER=<id>` | every eligible 8-live round on the third packed block (docs/tp4-octo3.md); the waiver id in the env is what admits it outside a gate |
| tile-aligned drafter window | `QWEN_FAST_TAP_WINDOW_ALIGNED=1` | data movement before any block runs (tp4/rebind-fast) |
| host KV tier, preconverted checkpoints | `QWEN_PREFIX_HOST_TIER_GIB=32`, `_AUDIT=0`, `_VERIFY=sample`, `QWEN_PREFIX_CKPT_PRECONVERTED=1`, `_AUDIT=0` | the tier's production setting (docs/prefix-store-hygiene.md); no kill-switch path, no instrument |
| the third block's memory plan | KV pool 19,968 to 16,500 blocks, trace region 512 to 640 MiB, `QWEN36_MAX_TOKENS_ALL_USERS` 1,055,488 | applied by `memory_plan: octo` (make_octo3_profiles.apply_memory_plan); margin 5 MB, the plan's named risk |

The tool-call whitespace fix is **not** in the table: it is default on for every profile (a correctness fix, not a lever).

**Not in the candidate:** the per-tenant quotas (`QWEN_PREFIX_TENANT_QUOTA_*`). The contract refuses them on a traffic profile until a qualification record exists (`prefix_policy_problems`); they stay a gate-only
instrument (job TQ1 on the audited twin). `QWEN_FAST_ADMISSION_DIAG_TRIM` stays excluded (docs/tp4-octo3.md section 7).

Differences between the candidates, mechanically (held by `test_ship4_profiles`): `-fx-traffic-s4` = `-fx-traffic-o3-oneq` + `QWEN_FAST_GRID13=prefill,drafter` + the five tier env lines, and nothing else (same engine, same
memory plan); it is also `-fx-traffic` + the octo, prefill, TAP and tier env + the memory plan.

## 3. Why one waiver, and what it says

One `owner_traffic_waiver` (id `ship4-fx-262k-2026-10-11`), decision **PENDING**, with the levers `serving_c2_contract` lets a waiver name: the four of the fusion ship waiver (`QWEN_FAST_TP4_SDPA=multi`, the F1 conv-gates
spread, engine reuse and its drafter book), the octo shape and its three levers and the minimum-live knob (`WAIVABLE_LEVERS`), and the two tier levers at their values (`TIER_WAIVABLE_LEVERS`). It lists, lettered
A to F, what each part is outside of: the inherited fusion waiver; the octo shape (no `packed_any_evidence_tp4.json` record covers flags 0x21 G8B1 or the B = 4 bundle, the batched GDN launch at eight users, the quarter-tile
movers; the octo draft's tau); the tier (outside any pinned record, never beside concurrent decode of other seats, never beside octo-T8 or a rebind of a parked engine on a restored turn, never in a 16,500-block pool);
the prefill SDPA (named as the owner's decision: its binary has its own records now); the grids (default-off, exact by argument and audit, switchable); TAP and the whitespace fix. It closes with: **nothing in the list has
run on a card together**; the audited attach A1, the ship gates and the tier pair of `references/fusion-jobs/ship4` are the first. The evidence list cites the octo-2 runs, the tier runs
(38082194508, 38082317009, 38082865675, 38083229148, 38084230415), the oneq records (RQ0-RQ2) and the whitespace round-trip, and ends with what has NOT run.

A profile whose decision does not start with APPROVED boots in a gate or a smoke (and logs the word), but no build bakes it (`c2_serving_job.read_bake`): B1 of the pack is refused until then, by design. Decide
it with `python3 scripts/ci/make_fusion_profiles.py --approve-ship "APPROVED by the owner ..." --ship traffic-s4`: it edits `fusion-wp/S4.json` and nothing else, then regenerates the profile (only
`owner_traffic_waiver.decision` of the candidate changes). Then the CPU suite on that commit, then the tag.

## 4. The switchable line

`QWEN_FAST_GRID13=prefill,drafter` is one line of the ship entry's `env` (the audited twin carries `=1`, which adds the verify sub-switch: the first audited attach of it, run 38116769673, stopped at engine start on an unresolved device before it compared anything, and the next one is its first audit). If the F2 decode ABAB (or the N1/N2 pair below, inside the whole stack) says LOSS, delete that line from `S4.json`, run `make_fusion_profiles.py --write`,
`make_ship4_profiles.py --write` (the no-grid twin then equals the candidate, so its parent check refuses: delete the twin and the N jobs in the same change) and `make_ship4_jobs.py --write`, and re-run A1 and G1 on the new
profile. The prefill SDPA line is separable the same way (its separate pack is `references/fusion-jobs/ship-traffic-o3` for the octo-only candidate, whose image does not need the K64j-OQ graft).
