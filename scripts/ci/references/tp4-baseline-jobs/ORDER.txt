# The order of the BASELINE development window: a short TT outage that runs ONLY on today's production image and production profile, before the big combined window (tp4-w2ln-jobs). One line per job:
# <template name without .env> <class> <image tag> <estimated minutes> <tag> <box minutes>. TARGET 6-8 h of outage, HARD CAP 9 h (540 min), A0 to Z. Nothing here builds an image, moves :latest or places a model.
# CLASSES. stop: the window halts at its first failure (the hand-back still runs). soft: data; a failure or a skip does not stop the window. opt: OPTIONAL, run only when the clock allows (below). hand: the hand-back, always runs.
# drv: a DRIVER step with no template and no tag (LM: the links re-measure workflow; TICK: the wait for the next topology tick).
# MINUTES are ESTIMATES: P1a-CTL, P1b-CTL, L8-CTL, C16-CTL, A0 and X0 are the combined pack's (tp4-w2ln-jobs), PROF-CTL is the profile pack's 130 min expected for four seats (80 at best, 190 at worst; the W2 pack carried 150 for eight users: no measured eight-seat run exists), V1a and V1b are the owner's
# 30 and 90, the hand-back is a reset 15 + the links re-measure 10 + up to one topology tick 18 (15-minute timer plus 3) + the agent start 15. BOXES: the docker timeout of a job's arms plus the per-arm overhead (c2_prefix_gate PLAN_ARMS with S2_TIMEOUTS, c2_serving_gate plan_arms,
# ops_profile_plan's arm seconds) or the card-M harness's own 3 h timeout: what the job can take when it runs to its limit. test_tp4_baseline_window computes every box from those tables. A '-' box is a short step with no gate timeout.
# OUTAGE ESTIMATE (the test pins these numbers): PLANNED 533 min = 8.9 h from A0 to Z (A0 X0 P1a P1b L8 C16 PROF ZR LM TICK Z). The OPTIONAL V5 block (R4 V1a V1b) is 135 min more: PLANNED + V5 = 668 min = 11.1 h, which exceeds the cap, so by estimate it never fits beside PROF-CTL.
# ALTERNATIVE (SKIP_JOBS=PROF-CTL-ops-profile-8x4k): the V5 block instead of the profile = 538 min = 9.0 h, inside the cap by 2 minutes (zero real margin: the owner picks one of the two before the window). CORE (stop after C16, no PROF, no V5) = 403 min = 6.7 h. A job that runs to its box can overrun its estimate (L8-CTL by up to 141 min, C16-CTL 108, P1b-CTL 78, PROF-CTL 46, P1a-CTL 33); the clock rule below is checked before each job, so after a hung job the later ones are SKIPPED-TIME and the hand-back runs.
# STOP-EARLY POINTS (the driver reads a file named STOP in its results directory before every job, and the hard cap before every job): after P1b-CTL (the prefix verdicts: 255 min = 4.2 h incl. A0 and X0), after C16-CTL (core, 345 min = 5.8 h), after PROF-CTL (475 min = 7.9 h). Stopping early skips the remaining jobs and runs the hand-back.
# THE CLOCK RULE (the driver): before each job, elapsed + the job's estimate + the hand-back estimate (58) must not pass the hard cap (540 min); else the job is SKIPPED-TIME. An opt block (R4 V1a V1b) needs the whole block (135) to fit. A job that was started runs to its end: nothing is cancelled
# (above all never a profile job: a cancel leaves root-owned logs). The driver ALERTS at 8 h of elapsed time.
# TAGS: experiment/c2-serving-vN, hardware-allowlisted and never pushed before (checked against the remote by the driver's preflight). The k-th job that has a tag takes the k-th tag of v538 upward; the reserve v550-v557 is for re-runs (an INFRA or RERUN no-verdict of P1a or P1b, a retried
# A0, ZR or Z, a re-run of a job whose runner died). The combined pack's own tag list (tp4-w2ln-jobs ORDER) starts at v538: it must drop the tags this window used.
# NO OTHER WINDOW DRIVER MAY BE ALIVE and no privileged builder container may be up. Every job that opens a gate or prefix step refuses to start while prefix-reuse.off or levern.off exists on the hub mount (the workflow's preflight step).
#
# PRECONDITIONS (the private preflight reads them; none is a card job): production healthy before A0 (the window takes it down on purpose); the hardware runner online and idle; the allowlist covering the tags; disk headroom on the rig; tp4-serve-10 present in the rig's Docker;
# the CPU suite green on the pushed branch; the owner has approved the outage and will be available at the hand-back (the operator needs the owner's admin key at the very end).
#
# DEPENDENCIES (machine-greppable): '# NEEDS <jobs> <- <jobs>' means the jobs on the left run only if every item on the right completed and passed its READ rule; otherwise the driver skips them.

# The prefix, gate and profile jobs open their own reset (C2_ACTIONS reset), so none of them needs another to have passed: a failed P1a-CTL (a production finding) does not skip P1b-CTL, L8-CTL, C16-CTL or PROF-CTL. A stop-class failure (A0, X0) skips everything but the hand-back.
#
# READ RULES. P1a-CTL and P1b-CTL: PASS = the arm verdict PASS and the read of their templates; FAIL = any FAIL (a PRODUCTION FINDING: ALERT, the owner decides after the window about prefix-reuse.off); anything else is NO-VERDICT with its reason (TIMEBOX, INFRA, NOT_EXERCISED, RERUN), which counts as NOT QUALIFIED
#  for any cutover. Only INFRA or RERUN is re-run once under a reserve tag; a TIMEBOX NO-VERDICT is not retried with the same box. L8-CTL and C16-CTL: the verdict of their gate plan (matrix, churn) and the read of their templates; FAIL is a production finding. PROF-CTL: ops-twin and ops-trace PASS, the report and
#  the compressed CSV in the artifact; a short report ('too few complete sessions') is data, not a fail. V1a and V1b: 'GDN_V5 verdict=PASS' (V1b: scope=full); FAIL or NO-DECISION stops the V5 block. A run that ends green is read in its log (the driver greps HANG SUSPECTED, audit mismatches, refused rounds,
#  watcher errors) and a hit on a stop job holds the window. The attach-time argv of every gate arm is read from its artifact before any number is trusted (memory: read the launched argv).
