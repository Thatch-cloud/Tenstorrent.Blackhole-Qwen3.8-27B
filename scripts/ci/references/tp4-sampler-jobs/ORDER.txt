# pinned sampler once in the packed warm forward only (QWEN_FAST_PACKED_SAMPLER_PREWARM=1), ST also records it inside the packed verify

# many packed rounds; concurrent4_v164order and concurrent4: the four-user tail). One stall restarts that arm's count and keeps the
# stall watch's triage output for the report.
