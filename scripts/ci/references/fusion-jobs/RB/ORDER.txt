# THE FINDING (measured on the container logs of four card runs, 217 rebinds; python3 scripts/ci/rebind_split.py reproduces it). A returning turn's bridge build

#   C rebind_slot 130-190 ms, D the memory ledger's after reading 60-70 ms, and B, adoption -> ledger 'before op=rebind', which is BIMODAL: 59-176 ms or 640-770 ms.
#   B holds the first capture.outputs(): the ttnn.concat of the drafter window's two pieces, whose heights (P-2048) mod 2048 and its complement change with every
#   prompt length. Unaligned pieces take ttnn's untilize / row-major concat / retilize path: 3 programs keyed by the piece shapes ('four-card engine programs'
#   +3 on 159 of 159 new unaligned splits, +1 on tile-aligned splits, +0 on a repeated length). All 91 slow rebinds were +3; 68 other +3 rebinds were fast (the
#   kernels already in the persistent per-graft kernel cache: inferred); 0 of 52 rebinds with +0/+1 were slow. The prefill step that took the snapshots pays too:
#   12 of 29 cold/hit twin pairs +187..+814 ms. The lever makes every shape before one final slice tile-aligned (fixed set) and that slice's input fixed (2080
#   rows, 31 offsets), so no new prompt length compiles anything once the set is cached.
