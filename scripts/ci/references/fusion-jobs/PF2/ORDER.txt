# WHAT THIS DECIDES (the owner's, never the window's): whether the last step of every split prompt should write the decode slot from device-side clones of the GDN scratch instead of reading the scratch to the host and uploading it back. Today the step pays 240 blocking reads and 240 uploads before 48 layers of slot writes; the PF1 device profile of the ship-4 stack measured 48.6 ms of idle device for the reads and 115.2 ms for the writes, 8.0 ms of it device kernels, on a profiled run (so the upper end of what production pays). The expected saving is 20 to 55 ms a final step, a few percent of a returning turn's 1.1 to 1.2 s step and about twice that once the first re-prefilled chunk is gone (tp4/exact-end); the expectation is arithmetic on that profile, not a measurement. A1 and AP1 are the exactness gates; T1-T4 and R1-R2 read the saving. Every result is UNQUALIFIED.
